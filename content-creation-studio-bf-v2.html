Two sources of posts. Pipeline posts came through the topic flow (Candidate → Draft → Delivery), so they carry a topic and its score. AI Studio posts were generated ad-hoc in the Creative Studio — no candidate, therefore no topic score. That is structural, not a gap waiting to be filled: the six components (Brand Fit, Lead Time, …) are all properties of a candidate.
Once the post-level score formula lands, both kinds will carry one — it measures the post itself and does not need a candidate. Origin is derived from whether the post has a parent delivery, so no extra field is required.
⚠ AI Studio posts also have no verified sources, no research pack and no section anchors, so fewer Readiness checks apply to them. The publish gate shows this honestly rather than making both look equally vetted.
