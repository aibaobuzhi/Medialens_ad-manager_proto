Between 17–19 Aug 2026, Hong Kong and Macau influencer and publisher pages in the top 25% engagement tier produced 871 posts across the Top 50 trending keywords, generating just over 1.0M engagements. Attention was heavily concentrated in hyper-local news moments — the window can be characterised as "locally-anchored, incident-driven, photo-led conversation with celebrity moments as the engagement multiplier."

Key Findings

Local incident cluster dominates volume

元朗 is the single largest keyword by both posts (89, 10.22% of all posts) and engagements (117,459, 11.70%), and it travels with a connected cluster — 漁護署 (58 posts / 81,509 engagements), YOHO (24 / 50,910) and 元朗YOHO (30 / 41,268) — indicating one localised news event powering four of the top five keywords.

Celebrity keywords convert attention most efficiently

Jennie averages 1,776 engagements per post and G-DRAGON follows the same pattern — far above the report-wide average of 1,153 — showing K-pop moments outperform on a per-post basis even with modest posting volume.

Publisher-style coverage is high-volume, lower-yield

Media-heavy keywords such as 文匯報 (30 posts, 400 average engagements) sit well below the overall average, a familiar publisher pattern of frequency over per-post depth.

Photo-led formats carry the window

Across the top keywords, photo is the dominant post type (e.g. 72 of 89 元朗 posts), with video concentrated in celebrity and event keywords — format choice tracks topic type rather than page habit.

Strategic Recommendations

  • Ride the incident cluster while it peaks: the 元朗 / 漁護署 / YOHO cluster is still the volume centre — content published against it inherits an audience 4× larger than the median keyword.
  • Prioritize celebrity angles for efficiency: Jennie-class keywords deliver ~1.5× the average engagements per post; a single well-timed post beats volume plays.
  • Replicate the photo-first format on local topics: photo posts dominate the highest-engagement local keywords; lead with strong stills before investing in video for this topic class.
