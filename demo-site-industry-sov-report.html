This is a fragmented, stories-led category where different advertisers lead on different metrics — no single player dominates every dimension.

Key Findings & Insights:

  • Share of Voice & Market Concentration: content share is unusually top-heavy while value share is not.
    • CurrentBody owns content SOV with 462 contents — 44.59% of the Top-10 pool — nearly 2.3× second-placed Merz Aesthetics (205, 19.79%).
    • Engagement SOV is a two-horse race: Merz Aesthetics (32.9K) edges CurrentBody (32.2K), with 24/7 Fitness (23.6K) third.
    • Estimated ad value SOV is far flatter: CurrentBody leads at just 18.14% (HK$2.1M) of the HK$11.3M Top-10 pool, with Merz (HK$1.8M) and Oasis Medical (HK$1.7M) close behind — high content volume has not translated into proportional value dominance.
  • Volume vs. Efficiency: CurrentBody wins on scale (462 contents at 185 avg engagements each), but 24/7 Fitness is the efficiency benchmark at 513 avg engagements per content — 2.8× CurrentBody's rate on one-seventh the volume. Brands entering this category should treat CurrentBody as the volume benchmark and 24/7 Fitness as the efficiency benchmark.
  • Content Format Mix: with Stories at 38.8% and Album at 32.2%, always-on lightweight formats carry this category; Video (21.8%) matters but is not the lead format. Video reach is concentrated: Oasis Medical alone drew 2.2M video views — an order of magnitude above most Top-10 peers.
  • Investment Intensity: the Top-10 estimated ad value pool is HK$11.3M. Staying visible at the top tier costs HK$1.3M–2.1M (perFACE through CurrentBody), while the entry ticket to the Top-10 sits near HK$325.4K (Invisalign) — a wide but climbable ladder.

Benchmarks & Strategic Recommendations:

  • Benchmark content cadence against CurrentBody's 462 contents per quarter if competing on share of feed, or against the 24/7 Fitness efficiency bar of 513 avg engagements per content if competing on quality (per Volume vs. Efficiency).
  • Prioritize Stories and Album formats in the content plan — together they carry 71% of category output — before scaling video (per Content Format Mix).
  • Target the HK$325.4K–556K estimated ad value band to enter the visible Top-10 tier, and budget HK$1.3M+ only if aiming for top-5 value share (per Investment Intensity).
  • Differentiate on engagement quality rather than volume: with value share fragmented (leader at 18.14%), a high-efficiency challenger can win engagement SOV without matching CurrentBody's output (per SOV & Market Concentration).
